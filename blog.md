## [Windows Defender False Positive on Launcher](https://open.mp/blog/launcher-false-positive)

February 12, 2025 · 2 min read

iAmir

itsneufox

Some users have reported that **Windows Defender is flagging the open.mp Launcher as a Trojan/virus**. This is a **false positive**, the launcher is completely safe to use.

The launcher is **fully open source** and you can review or build it yourself: [https://github.com/openmultiplayer/launcher](https://github.com/openmultiplayer/launcher)

We're **already working on fixing this** so future builds won't be flagged.
